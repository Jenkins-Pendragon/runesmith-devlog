After the [last three posts]({{base}}/posts/falling-from-olympus/) I sat down and played the demo from the very start, as a new player would. A lot of what I found wasn't broken, just confusing: too many buttons on day one, quests that assumed you knew where things were, and a prologue that ended a little abruptly. This post is about that first hour.

## The prologue, round two

The Zeus fight got another pass:

- **Blade locks have two grades now**, Good and Perfect. A Perfect makes Zeus stagger.
- **Zeus leaves a short opening** after each of his attacks, about a second, and that's when the game suggests your next skill instead of in the middle of his swing.
- **Phases end early** if you deal enough damage, so a player who fights well isn't kept waiting.
- **Subtitles have a soft background**, so they're readable against the bright Olympus sky.
- **The ending changed.** The long fall through the clouds is gone. Zeus kicks you off the island, you fly out of frame, and the screen goes black after his last line. You wake up in the meadow.
- **New settings:** "Always Skip Prologue" and a separate volume for voices. You can also skip from the pause menu, with a confirmation.

Follow the lit path from where you land and you reach the old statues of the gods. Zeus speaks to you there one more time, now fully voiced:

> "Three of your lights wait in this stone. Take one. Only one."

That's where you pick your first god's gift.

## One thing at a time

The HUD used to show everything from the first second: sonar, the return-to-hideout spell, the weapon button. Now they unlock as the story gets to them:

- **Sonar** unlocks when you claim your first statue quest.
- **Return to Hideout** unlocks when a quest first asks you to go home.
- **The weapon button** unlocks when you equip your sword.

Each unlock gets its moment. A copy of the button appears in the middle of the screen with its name, like "HIDEOUT UNLOCKED", rises, flies to its place on the bar and lights up. If something else is going on, it waits for a quiet moment.

![The return-home button in the middle of the screen under "HIDEOUT UNLOCKED", about to fly to the ability bar]({{base}}/assets/img/feet-unlock.jpg)

The ability bar also got a fifth button for the Quick Menu, and the pause menu shows a strip of key hints at the bottom, for the features you've unlocked so far.

## Hermes comes first

The first statue you meet after the fall is now Hermes. Touch it and the messenger god opens his path and gives you back the memory of his sandals: **Hermes Boots**.

![The player in front of the Statue of Hermes in the meadow]({{base}}/assets/img/feet-hermes-statue.jpg)

Hermes Boots is a skill you switch on and off. While it's on, you run faster and leave afterimages behind you, with golden trails at your feet, sparks where they land and small wings at your heels. It drains runic energy every second and stops the bar from refilling, and it switches itself off the moment you enter combat. It's a tool for the road, not for fighting. The next quest, Winged Steps, has you unlock it in the tree and try it once.

## A quest line with fewer dead ends

Playing it fresh showed me where the quests lost people:

- **Red dots on recipes.** When the active quest asks for something you can craft, the recipe gets a small red dot in every bench that makes it. The Craft key's icon gets one too, until you open the window.
- **Item names match.** Quest text now uses the exact names items have in the game, in all 33 languages. No more looking for a "Mining Axe" when the game calls it a Basic Pickaxe.
- **You build your own forge.** The free, ready-made forge in the hideout is gone. A new quest, Raising the Furnace, has you build an Ore Furnace and smelt Purified Clay, and then Birth of the Runes has you craft the Rune Furnace yourself and place it.
- **The Cyclops quest was split in two.** First you craft a Swift Temper and rub it into your weapon, then you go after the Cyclops' eye. The trial at Ares' statue now carries the offering you used to make at a separate altar, so there's one less errand.
- **Campfires are home base.** You can have one campfire, and building a new one moves it. When you die, you get up next to it, and when you come back to the map, you start beside it.

## Feedback that tells you what happened

A few small screens that make a big difference:

- **The pickup feed merges** the same item into one line. It counts up with a small gold punch instead of stacking ten rows of "Wood".
- **Leveling up** fills the bar to the end, flashes it gold and shows "+1 Skill Point". If you have points you haven't spent, a badge says so.
- **When you die,** a card tells you what happened: how much level progress you lost, what killed you, and a countdown until you get back up.

![Two cards side by side: the "You Fell" death card with level progress lost, the cause and a countdown, and a gold "+1 Skill Point" over the level bar]({{base}}/assets/img/feet-cards.jpg)

## How the demo ends

When you reach the end of the demo, you now get two ways to see where the game is going:

- **Try the Anvil** raises your forge to level 5 and gives you a pouch of rune dust, once per realm. Level 5 is where Mythic runes start to appear.
- **Try the Weapon Pack** gives you a starter weapon from every family, bow and shield included.

Next to them, a **Your Build** card shows the runes you have equipped, your best rune and how many of the five powers Zeus took from you you've earned back.

![The demo end screen: Thanks for playing, a Your Build card, three cards about the full game, and Try the Anvil and Try the Weapon Pack buttons]({{base}}/assets/img/feet-demo-end.jpg)

## 33 languages, for real this time

Every new line from the last few weeks, from the quest chain and the prologue to the statues, achievements and settings, is now translated into all 33 languages instead of falling back to English. That's 295 new lines in 31 languages. Headings also pick the right font per language now, so alphabets that the heading font doesn't cover still look like headings.
