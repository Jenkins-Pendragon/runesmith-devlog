Wild plants were the starting point. Harvesting supplied items and effects, but I wanted a field to become somewhere the player could tend, rather than another resource to empty and leave behind.

## Choosing what to grow

Crop and seed definitions gained names and icons. A farming camera and socket grid supported moving around the field, while the seed-selection screen tracked the seeds available in the inventory and placed the selected crop.

[![Small, medium and large farm plots]({{base}}/assets/img/archive-farm-plots.jpg)]({{base}}/assets/img/archive-farm-plots.jpg)
*Small, medium and large farm plots. Staged current-prefab illustration, captured October 2, 2026. Crop examples are arranged for the image; scale and placement are illustrative.*

The plot comparison makes a small detail easier to see: the beds themselves have different footprints. I staged crop prefabs in the current small, medium and large beds for this illustration. It is an asset study, not a time-lapse or a demonstration of crop growth.

## Tending the field

Water, fertilizer and blessings became inputs to growth. Harvesting and farming state were carried through saves, so leaving the game did not mean treating the field as a blank slate on the next visit.

[![Cooking workbench variants]({{base}}/assets/img/archive-cooking.jpg)]({{base}}/assets/img/archive-cooking.jpg)
*Cooking workbench variants. Staged current-prefab comparison, captured October 2, 2026. Left to right: tier 1, tier 2, tier 3.*

The cooking station is part of the wider crafting context around those ingredients. Its current tiers grow from a compact setup into a fuller workstation, with storage and equipment making the progression visible.

This phase joined gathering to a longer rhythm: choose a seed, care for the crop, then come back for the harvest. The field was beginning to have its own place in the crafting loop.
