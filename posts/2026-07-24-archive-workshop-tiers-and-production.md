A bench upgrade should be visible before you open its menu. The current prefabs contain three tier views for each of these ten stations, so I arranged them at the same angle to make the differences easy to compare.

These images were captured on October 2, 2026 using the current prefab variants. They illustrate the workshop's progression; they are not screenshots from the historical builds covered by this entry.

## An upgrade you can see

The historical tier work linked a bench's level to its active visual variant. Production tiers also affected crafting speed, inventory slots and recipe availability. The model change was one part of a practical upgrade, rather than a separate cosmetic reward.

## Explaining what the bench can make

Crafting panels gained multiple-output recipes, tier-dependent output chances and visible locks. Craftable-only filtering preserved the selected recipe and quantity when materials changed. Queue details stayed open, and the unused SpeedUp control was hidden.

Benches opened their inventory beside the player bag and preserved a bag that was already open. Upgrade costs refreshed with material changes. Crafted items and quest rewards retained separate item instances, and the default god-path gate for rune crafting was disabled in the later work of this period.

## Arranging a working hideout

Placement previews and saved positions let benches and props become an arrangement the player could keep. Dismantling checked occupied or busy objects and handled refunds. The grinder added an item list, dust preview, pausing, refunds and a saved queue.

Production queues saved on change and progressed while the player was elsewhere or offline, within configured limits. Transfer handling respected who had opened the player bag. Queue-cost fixes protected production from recycled interface state.

Hideout mockups, campsite rocks, furnace and alchemy art, potion assets, raw materials, cooked-food definitions and recipe icons filled out the production setting. Ore targeting and classification, animal and boss art, habitat skins and spawner prefabs were prepared alongside that content; not every imported asset represented a completed encounter.
